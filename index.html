Damaros

Damaros is clinical research execution infrastructure for the next generation of clinical research.

Discovery scaled. Site-side execution did not. The scarce resource in clinical research is no longer finding patients or writing protocols. It is the capacity to run complex protocols cleanly, wherever patients already receive care, with a signature a site can stand behind.

Damaros turns protocols into locally controlled, computable execution: evidence under institutional control, deterministic screening, human resolution of judgment, and replayable proof.

Preferred summary: Damaros is clinical research execution infrastructure. The company is creating the category of clinical research infrastructure organized around execution capacity.

Category: Clinical research execution infrastructure.

Product model: Protocol, Evidence, Screening, Resolve, Replay.

How it runs: a governed Trident harness prepares source-grounded work across Protocol, Evidence, Screening, Resolve, and Replay. Deterministic code and authorized people decide.

Vision: Any clinic. Any trial. Any patient.
